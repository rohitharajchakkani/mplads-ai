# Executive attention

Signal Count is calculated as: open review cases (including assigned, under-review, and reopened) + follow-up cases + active escalations + high/very-high alerts + high/very-high risk works.

Attention precedence is exact: **HIGH** if Signal Count is at least 10, active escalations are at least 2, or open cases are at least 5 and high-priority alerts are at least 5. Otherwise it is **MODERATE** for Signal Count 3–9, one active escalation, or at least two open cases. It is **LOWER** only when Signal Count is below 3 and there are no active escalations. Drivers are returned with their actual counts and drill-down targets.
